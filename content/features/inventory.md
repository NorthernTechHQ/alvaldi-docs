On the right side of the device info page, you can find information about the device:

{{< wideimg "/screenshot-gui-device-info-page-inventory.png" "Screenshot showing device inventory in Alvaldi's device info page">}}

The information is organized into 2 tabs; inventory and identity.
Inventory information such as operating system, device type and version(s) help give you some context about the device you're looking at and what might be causing issues.
